# Public Rubric

We do not expect one official answer. We are looking for judgment under ambiguity.

## Build Judgment

Did you choose a small, useful, finishable scope?

## Engineering Fundamentals

Does the artifact run? Is the code or structure understandable? Does it handle messy data reasonably?

## Product Thinking

Is the output useful to a teammate? Did you optimize for one clear use case?

## Messy-Data Comfort

Did you notice missing values, inconsistent labels, duplicated rows, suspicious spikes, or caveats in the notes?

## Communication

Can someone quickly understand what you built, what assumptions you made, and what you would do next?

## AI Collaboration

If you used AI, did you use it intentionally and critically? Did you verify important parts yourself?
