# Collaborator Brief

This repo is a starting point for a lightweight Data Science Intern build challenge.

The main thing to preserve is the signal: can this person quickly build something small, useful, and well-judged from messy data, then explain their decisions clearly?

## What We Are Looking For

We are trying to find candidates with taste, judgment, speed, and messy-data comfort.

A strong submission should make us think:

- they picked a smart scope;
- they built something a teammate could actually use;
- they noticed data weirdness without getting stuck;
- they made reasonable assumptions and named them;
- they used AI, if they used it, as a thought partner rather than blindly outsourcing the work;
- they shipped something coherent instead of trying to impress with too much scope.

## What This Should Not Become

Please avoid turning this into:

- a long take-home assignment;
- a SQL trivia test;
- a LeetCode-style puzzle;
- a legal-tech knowledge test;
- a broad analytics report;
- a full ML/modeling project;
- a UI polish contest;
- a challenge where there is one hidden correct answer.

The sweet spot is not solving a hard problem. The sweet spot is making something small, useful, and well-judged under ambiguity.

## Dataset Direction

The dataset should stay fictional, small, and somewhat messy.

Good kinds of messiness:

- missing values;
- duplicated rows;
- inconsistent labels or casing;
- suspicious spikes;
- small-sample caveats;
- notes that change interpretation;
- metrics that invite sanity checks.

The dataset should give candidates room to show judgment, but it should not require domain knowledge or a long setup.

## AI Collaboration Signal

The AI note is important. We are not trying to catch people using AI. We are trying to understand how they work with it.

Useful signal includes:

- what they asked AI to help with;
- where AI sped them up;
- what they verified themselves;
- where they disagreed with or overrode AI;
- whether they can explain their own decisions after using AI.

Avoid making the AI note feel performative or like a confession. It should feel like a short work-log.

## Review Taste

Prefer the candidate who builds one clear, useful thing and explains the tradeoffs over the candidate who starts five impressive things and finishes none.

Prefer practical judgment over polish.
Prefer evidence of thinking over buzzwords.
Prefer a useful artifact over a long report.
Prefer curiosity and clarity over perfect credentials.
